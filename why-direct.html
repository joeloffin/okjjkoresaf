Why owners sell directly

Direct to JKO Land Investments vs. a traditional listing

A listing can make sense if you want to market widely and wait for the right buyer. A direct sale makes sense if you want a defined path, fewer moving parts, and a closing date you can plan around.

Sell your land

Direct to JKO Land Investments

  • No agent commission
  • No public listing or showings
  • A defined closing timeline
  • Clear offer before you commit
  • Standard closing costs covered*
  • Land is purchased to be built on, not held

Traditional listing

  • Commission may reduce proceeds
  • Photos, marketing, and showings
  • Timing depends on a buyer
  • Negotiations may continue
  • Additional transaction costs may apply
  • No certainty the next owner will build

*Subject to the purchase agreement and property review. Closing time depends on title status, seller readiness, and closing-provider requirements.
