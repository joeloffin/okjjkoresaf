Why Sell to JKO Directly Instead of a Realtor — and How to Keep the Sale Secure

June 18, 2026 · 4 min read

Realtors help many sellers market a property and negotiate with retail buyers. For vacant land or a property you simply want sold cleanly, listing is not always the best fit. Selling directly to JKO Land Investments trades a long marketing cycle for a defined cash offer — without giving up a professional, secure closing.

Why owners choose a direct sale to JKO

  • No listing commission — the offer is not reduced by agent fees.
  • No showings or open houses — especially helpful if you live far from the lot.
  • A timeline you can plan around — typical closings in 21–30 days when title is clear.
  • Clear terms up front — review the number before you commit; no pressure.
  • Purpose after the sale — we buy so land can move toward homes, not sit in a long hold.

A realtor listing can still make sense if you want maximum market exposure and are willing to wait. Direct is for owners who value certainty and simplicity. See our full comparison on why sell direct.

How to keep a land sale secure

“Cash” should never mean wiring money to a stranger or signing without title work. A secure sale uses the same professional closing rails as any serious real estate deal:

  • A written purchase agreement with clear terms
  • Earnest money held by a neutral closing professional — not by the buyer personally
  • Title search and curing of legitimate issues
  • Closing documents prepared and explained by licensed counsel
  • Funds disbursed at closing when conditions are met

We close with a licensed title attorney or title company

JKO Land Investments does not close land purchases “handshake style.” Every deal runs through a licensed title company or closing attorney in your state. That professional:

  • Holds earnest money in escrow
  • Examines title and flags problems early
  • Prepares the deed and closing package
  • Disburses your proceeds at closing

You are always free to have your own attorney review the contract. Using a title attorney or title company is how both sides stay protected — and it is the only way we close.

Bottom line

Selling to JKO directly means fewer middle steps and a cash path built for unused land. Security comes from the same place it should in any sale: a licensed title professional standing between you and the wire. That is how we do every closing in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia.
