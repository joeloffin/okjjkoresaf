How to Sell Vacant Land Quickly — Without Agent Hassle, for Pure Cash

October 14, 2025 · 3 min read

Vacant land can be hard to list the traditional way. Buyers are fewer, showings are awkward, and commissions cut into what you walk away with. If you want speed and certainty more than a long market campaign, a direct cash sale is often the cleaner path.

What “pure cash” actually means

A cash buyer funds the purchase without a mortgage contingency. That removes lender timelines and appraisal delays that can stall a listed deal. You still close through a licensed title company or attorney — cash is about how the buyer pays, not skipping proper closing.

Skip the agent hassle

Listing usually means photos, marketing, price reductions, and waiting for the right buyer to appear. A direct sale to a land investor cuts those steps: you review a clear offer, decide if the number and timeline work, and move toward closing when you are ready. No open houses. No commission carved out of the proceeds.

A simple process that moves

  1. Share the basics — address or parcel ID, and any known issues (access, taxes, heirs).
  2. Review a cash offer — know the price and timeline before you commit.
  3. Close professionally — earnest money is held by a licensed title professional; you get paid at closing when title is clear.

At JKO Land Investments, that path typically takes 21–30 days when title is clear — in North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia.

When a quick cash sale makes sense

  • You inherited a lot nobody plans to build on
  • You live out of state and do not want a long listing
  • Taxes or upkeep are costing more than the land is worth to you
  • You want a defined closing date you can plan around
