How it works

Three straightforward steps

No listing, showings, or open-ended wait for a buyer. You agree to a clear purchase. We close through a licensed professional. You receive the funds — typically in 21–30 days when title is clear.

Sell your land
  1. 01

    Agree on the purchase

    We review the property, answer your questions, and sign a clear agreement to purchase your land. You have time to read the terms before you sign. There is no listing, no commission, and no obligation until you agree.

  2. 02

    Secure the closing

    We place the agreed earnest-money deposit with a licensed title company or closing attorney in your state. That professional holds the process — not a handshake and a hope.

  3. 03

    Receive your payment

    The closing professional verifies title, prepares documents, and pays you the full agreed amount at closing. When title is clear, this is typically 21–30 days from agreement.
